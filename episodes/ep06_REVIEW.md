**Listenability.** Like Ep 5, this is a table of contents: each parva is a string of short plot clauses, read mostly
by one voice. It is unabridged. Four lines are spoken in character (see below), about half a minute together, so
the catalogue does break once at Krishna's offer and once in the night attack. Narration stays even and measured,
with the direction "reading the table of contents of a great epic; even, unhurried, each item distinct" through
Virata, Udyoga and the war books. Sauptika and Stri are solemn. The music gives the list its shape: forest ashram
for the disguise, grave under Kichaka and the cattle raids, tender under Uttara's marriage; royal under Krishna's
offer; sacred under the peace embassy and the Gita; grave through the war, the mace fight and the night attack;
tender under the women's lament, grave on the iron statue, Gandhari's wrath, the unburied dead and the cremation.

- **Krishna** speaks the offer at Upaplavya ("O ye first of men, a counsellor in myself who will not fight…") and,
  in Sauptika, "This shall not be." Bare "Krishna" in this span is Vasudeva throughout. Nothing here is Draupadi.
  The builder had left both lines unresolved, because bare "Krishna" is an ambiguous alias and is not used for
  speaker matching. Overrides assign them.
- **Aswatthaman** speaks the vow ("without killing all the Panchalas… I will not take off armour") and the weapon
  ("This is for the destruction of all the Pandavas"). "Vowed" is not in the builder's speech-verb list, so the
  vow was split out by hand; the parts rejoin to the KMG sentence. There is no `voices/aswatthaman/ref.wav`.
  The registry brief on the character ("a young Indian warrior, bright sharp tenor") is grounded in the birth
  and the training (Book 1 §131–§144). These two lines are the night attack. A brief for them needs your ear
  before any design. If you would rather keep both lines in Sauti's mouth, say so and the overrides come off.
- **The joint sentence** ("You should render us assistance in this war") is spoken by Duryodhana and Arjuna
  together. The builder gave it to Duryodhana, the first name. Under the crowd-voice rule Sauti reads it
  (commanding, one sentence). Giving it to Duryodhana would need a new voice and would leave Arjuna out.
- Narrator tags stay with Sauti: "and said,", "replied,", "vowed,", "saying,", "Then Krishna saying,".

## OCR / print repairs (spoken text only via `tts_replace`, flagged `ocr_fix`; KMG text untouched)
These are in our text and in the Gutenberg Adi Parva. The repair is the spelling KMG uses elsewhere in that file,
or, for the sloka count, the only reading that is a number.

- ¶14 "the story of **Damvodvava**" → **Dambhodbhava**. One occurrence, immediately before Matali and Galava,
  which is that Udyoga story. The name-list in the same book spells Dambhodbhava. The letters are badly garbled
  (b/v, a dropped h). *Say if you would rather keep "Damvodvava".*
- ¶14 "the high-souled **Matuli**" → **Matali**. ¶8 of this section says Matali; this is the only "Matuli" in the book.
- ¶15 "placing **Shikandin** before him" → **Sikhandin** (KMG's spelling on the other six occurrences).
- ¶16 "the elephant **Supritika**" → **Supratika**, Bhagadatta's elephant. *Less certain.* Gutenberg has Supritika
  in this one sentence. Supritika in §29 is a different person, the brother who was cursed into an elephant.
  Say if you would rather keep Supritika here.
- ¶16 "of **Shomadatta**" → **Somadatta** (fourteen other occurrences; this is the only Shomadatta).
- ¶17 "then that of **Darda**" → **Danda**, beside Dandasena. *Less certain:* one occurrence, an n/r misreading.
  Say if you would rather keep Darda.
- ¶17 "nine hundred and **sixty-tour**" → **sixty-four**. "Tour" is not a number. The Karna-parva count is 4,964.
- ¶19 "including **Drishtadyumna**" → **Dhrishtadyumna**. The same paragraph spells it Dhrishtadyumna a few lines later
  (thirty-four times in the book; this is the only Drishtadyumna).

Spoken-form tidying (not typos; hyphens make VoxCPM2 spell or stall): Sanat-sujata → Sanatsujata,
warrior-in-chariot → warrior in chariot, warriors-in-chariot → warriors in chariot.

The builder left the closing quote on Aswatthaman's weapon line (`Pandavas'`), because the next tag begins at the
semicolon and the quote then looks like a possessive. The spoken line drops that quote. The KMG text in the script
still shows it.

## Pronunciation risks
Twenty-seven names in the spoken text have no lexicon row: Alambusha, Amba, Bhagadatta, Bidula, Dambhodbhava, Danda,
Dandasena, Dussasana, Galava, Jalasandha, Jambu, Kichaka, Maharathas, Matali, Pandya, Sansaptakas, Sanatsujata,
Sauptika, Satyaki, shami (printed in lower case, the tree), Somadatta, Srutayus, Supratika, Trigartas, Tripura,
Uluka, Upaplavya.

Nine more are in the lexicon and still unverified: Aishika, Akshauhini, Hastinapura, Kshatriya, Kurus, Parasara,
Stri, Udyoga, Yoga. Aishika did pass on Sauti in the Ep 1 harness; the row was left provisional.
